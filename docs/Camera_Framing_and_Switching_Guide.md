# FLAG Church - Camera Framing & Switching Guidelines
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Applicable to:** Camera Operators & Video Switcher / Director

---

## 1. Fundamentals of Camera Framing

### A. Head Room
* **Rule:** Leave a small, natural amount of space between the top of the subject's head and the top of the video frame.
* **Avoid:** Too much headroom (subject sinks to the bottom half of the screen) or cutting off the forehead during normal speaking.

```
+------------------------------------+
|            [ 1-2 inches ]          |  <-- Natural Headroom
|               ( o.o )              |
|              /   |   \             |
|             /    |    \            |
|            [ Chest Line ]          |  <-- Ideal Medium Close-Up
+------------------------------------+
```

### B. Look Room / Lead Room
* If the speaker is looking toward stage left, place them slightly to the right of the frame, looking across the open space.
* Never place a speaker against the frame boundary they are speaking directly towards.

### C. Rule of Thirds
* Place the speaker's eyes along the upper horizontal third-line of the frame for natural, engaging eye contact with the viewer.

---

## 2. Standard Camera Roles & Shot Types

| Camera | Position | Primary Shot Type | Movement Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camera 1** | Center Balcony or Rear Center | Wide establishing shot (Full Stage) | Locked down on tripod. Slow, deliberate pans only during altar calls or wide congregation shots. |
| **Camera 2** | Center Floor / Elevated Platform | Medium Close-Up (Waist to Head) & Close-Up (Chest to Head) | Follows the main speaker/worship leader smoothly. Keep subject centered and focused. |
| **Camera 3** | Side Stage / 45-Degree Angle | Side Profile, Musician Solos, Congregation Reaction | Creative angles. Depth of field / bokeh shots of guitarists, pianist hands, choir, or hands lifted in worship. |

---

## 3. Video Switching Philosophy

### During Praise & Fast Worship:
* **Pace:** Energetic, rhythmic switching.
* **Transition Type:** Hard cuts (no dissolves) directly on the downbeat or musical change.
* **Angles:** Move between Cam 1 (Wide stage), Cam 3 (Drummer / Guitarist / Bassist), and Cam 2 (Worship Leader).

### During Deep Worship & Slow Songs:
* **Pace:** Slow, deliberate, prayerful.
* **Transition Type:** Smooth Cross-Dissolve (800ms - 1200ms).
* **Rule:** Hold shots longer (10 - 20 seconds). Avoid rapid cuts that disrupt prayer and personal connection with God.

### During the Sermon / Preaching:
* **Primary Shot:** 70% of preaching time should be on **Cam 2 (Medium Close-Up of Preacher)**.
* **Secondary Shot:** 20% on **Cam 3 (Side Angle / Profile)** when the pastor moves across the stage or looks towards that wing.
* **Context Shot:** 10% on **Cam 1 (Wide Stage)** when the pastor references a large illustration, gestures broadly, or walks across the altar.
* **Strict Rule:** Never cut to a camera that is searching for focus, zooming, or panning. The camera operator must lock the shot *before* the director takes it.

---

## 4. Director Intercom / Comms Commands Protocol

Clear communication prevents on-air mistakes. Every shot call must follow the **"Ready... Take"** structure:

```
Step 1: Director gives warning:
   "Ready Camera 2 on Pastor"
   
Step 2: Camera 2 operator frames, locks focus, and holds steady.

Step 3: Director executes transition:
   "Take Camera 2!" (or "Dissolve to Camera 2!")
```

### Key Director Commands:
* *"Ready Cam 1 on Wide... Take Cam 1."*
* *"Cam 2, push in tighter (slow zoom in) to chest level."*
* *"Cam 3, find me the keyboard player's hands... Ready Cam 3... Take Cam 3."*
* *"Standby graphics: Scripture John 3:16... Take lower third."*
* *"Drop lower third."*
* *"Hold steady, Cam 2, you are live."*

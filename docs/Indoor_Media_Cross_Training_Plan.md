# FLAG Church - Indoor Media Cross-Training Curriculum
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Program Director:** Lokesh (Indoor Media Team Leader)  
**Objective:** Cross-train all volunteers to eliminate single points of failure and prevent burnout.

---

## 1. The Strategy: "Every Member Knows Two Roles"

Currently in our roster:
* **Praveen** is operating alone for 4 consecutive 2nd services.
* **Joshua** is the only person on Spot Editing.
* **Andrew & Franklin** are handling 3rd service solo.

To build a resilient, confident media team, our goal over the next 4 weeks is for **every volunteer to master their primary role and shadow/learn a secondary role**.

---

## 2. 4-Week Saturday Training Schedule (1.5 Hours / Session)

```
[ Week 1 ] Fundamentals of Broadcast Audio & Cable Management
     │
[ Week 2 ] Camera Dynamics, Gimbal Balancing & Proper Framing
     │
[ Week 3 ] Video Switcher Operations (ATEM / vMix / OBS) & Comms
     │
[ Week 4 ] Spot Editing, Captions & Emergency Troubleshooting
```

### Week 1: Clean Sound & Reliable Power
* **Target Volunteers:** All team members.
* **Trainer:** Lokesh & Broadcast Sound Engineer.
* **Practical Drills:**
  1. Learn how to wrap XLR, HDMI, and SDI cables using the professional over-under technique.
  2. Setting up the ambient room mics and testing congregation sound in headphones.
  3. Verifying broadcast stream levels (-18 dBFS to -12 dBFS).
  4. Camera battery charging, pairing spare batteries, and SD card in-camera formatting.

### Week 2: Camera Operation, Angles & Gimbal Balancing
* **Target Volunteers:** Andrew, Franklin, Sam Davit, Yashwant, Balaji.
* **Trainer:** Lokesh.
* **Practical Drills:**
  1. Setting up tripod friction head for smooth pans and tilts.
  2. Framing exercises: Maintaining proper headroom and lead room on a moving speaker.
  3. 3-Axis mechanical balancing of the gimbal before powering on the motors.
  4. Responding to director comms calls ("Ready Cam 2... Take Cam 2").

### Week 3: Live System & Video Switching
* **Target Volunteers:** Praveen, Franklin, Andrew, Sam Davit.
* **Trainer:** Anand, Yashwant, Shyam Davit.
* **Practical Drills:**
  1. Switching between Cam 1 (Wide), Cam 2 (Tight), and Cam 3 (Dynamic).
  2. Triggering lower-third lyric overlays and scripture verses.
  3. Starting/stopping live streams and verifying local SSD backup recording.
  4. Simulated emergency: Handling a disconnected camera feed without panicking on air.

### Week 4: Spot Editing & Rapid Handoff
* **Target Volunteers:** Sam Davit, Franklin, Andrew.
* **Trainer:** Joshua.
* **Practical Drills:**
  1. Setting up a 9:16 vertical sequence in CapCut / Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve.
  2. Cutting a 45-second sermon highlight from the Sunday master recording.
  3. Generating auto-subtitles, highlighting keywords in yellow, and applying the FLAG logo.
  4. Exporting and uploading to the shared church drive.

## 4. Graduation & Recognition
* At the end of the 4 weeks, recognize volunteers during a team dinner or Sunday morning huddle.
* Volunteers who master both Camera and Live System earn the **"Senior Broadcaster"** badge, allowing them to lead 2nd or 3rd service independently.

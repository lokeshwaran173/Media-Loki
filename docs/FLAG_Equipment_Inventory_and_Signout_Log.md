## 3. Return & Inspection Protocol
When returning equipment:
1. **Physical Inspection:** Check camera body, lens front and rear elements for scratches, dirt, or moisture.
2. **Component Check:** Verify lens cap, tripod quick-release plate, battery, and SD card are all returned.
3. **Card Ingestion & Clearance:** Confirm footage shot during external shoots has been transferred to the editing drive.
4. **Recharging:** Batteries must be placed immediately on the charging dock so they reach 100% before Sunday morning.

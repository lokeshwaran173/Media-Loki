# FLAG Church - Indoor Media Operations Manual
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Department:** Indoor Media  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Target:** Production Excellence, Spiritual Focus, Distraction-Free Worship

---

## 1. Vision & Ministry Purpose
The Indoor Media Team at Full Life Centre AG Church exists to **remove distractions and amplify the Gospel**. 

Every camera angle, slide transition, and audio adjustment is an act of worship and service. Our goal is to ensure that both the physical congregation in the sanctuary and the online congregation joining from around the world experience God's presence seamlessly without technical hindrances.

---

## 2. Team Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Core Responsibilities | Key Skills Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Media Director / Switcher** | Calls camera shots, switches video feeds, monitors stream health, oversees the timing of the service. | Quick decision-making, musical rhythm, calm under pressure. |
| **Camera 1 Operator (Center / Wide)** | Covers stage master shot, full worship team, wide altar calls, and smooth slow pans. | Steady hands, smooth zooming, spatial awareness. |
| **Camera 2 Operator (Tight / Lead)** | Close-up shots of preacher (chest-up), worship leader, scripture readers, and soloists. | Smooth manual tracking, precise focus control. |
| **Camera 3 Operator (Dynamic / Roaming / Side)** | 45-degree angle of stage, keyboardist/drummer close-ups, congregation worship expressions. | Creativity, artistic framing, situational sensitivity. |
| **Visuals & Presentation Operator** | Operates ProPresenter / EasyWorship for song lyrics, scripture readings, countdown timers, and video playback. | Attention to spelling, lyric timing, active listening. |
| **Broadcast Audio Technician** | Monitors dedicated livestream audio mix, balancing vocals, instruments, and congregation room microphones. | Critical listening on flat studio headphones. |

## 3. Sunday Service Timeline & Master Checklist

```
[ T - 90 min ] Arrival & Team Prayer
      │
[ T - 75 min ] Hardware Power-Up & Cable Check
      │
[ T - 60 min ] Camera Matching (Colors, White Balance, Focus)
      │
[ T - 45 min ] Audio & Mic Soundcheck with FOH
      │
[ T - 30 min ] Presentation & Scripture Sync
      │
[ T - 15 min ] Pre-Service Countdown Goes Live on Stream
      │
[ T - 00 min ] SERVICE COMMENCES
      │
[ Post-Service ] Stop Stream, Backup Files, Equipment Safe-Down
```

### Phase 1: Pre-Service (T - 90 to T - 15 mins)
- [ ] **Arrival & Prayer (T-90):** Gather team for 5 minutes of scripture reading and prayer.
- [ ] **Power Sequencing (T-75):**
  1. Power on Video Switcher (ATEM / vMix PC).
  2. Power on Streaming & Presentation PCs.
  3. Power on Camera bodies and monitors.
- [ ] **Camera Matching (T-60):**
  - All cameras set to uniform frame rate (e.g., 1080p @ 50fps or 60fps).
  - Shutter speed locked (1/100 for 50fps, 1/125 for 60fps).
  - White balance calibrated using a white card on stage under full stage lights (e.g., 4500K - 5600K).
- [ ] **Broadcast Audio Verification (T-45):**
  - Wear closed-back studio monitor headphones at the media desk.
  - Verify worship leader mic, preacher wireless headset, and ambient room mics.
  - Confirm broadcast audio levels peak between **-18 dBFS and -12 dBFS** (never in the red / 0 dBFS).
- [ ] **Visuals / Presentation Check (T-30):**
  - Verify lyrics against worship team setlist.
  - Pre-load preacher's sermon slides, sermon title, and Bible scripture translations.
  - Test lower-third overlays onto the live video feed.
- [ ] **Livestream Initialization (T-15):**
  - Start stream broadcast on YouTube / Facebook with countdown loop and background music.
  - Verify stream health in YouTube Studio (Bitrate stable, green status, no dropped frames).

### Phase 2: In-Service Execution
- [ ] Maintain constant intercom discipline.
- [ ] Switch on the beat during praise & worship; hold steady shots during prayer and communion.
- [ ] Display song lyrics 1-2 beats *before* the line is sung so the church can sing along effortlessly.
- [ ] Display scripture lower-thirds immediately when the pastor cites a verse.

### Phase 3: Post-Service (T + 0 to T + 30 mins)
- [ ] Stop livestream 60 seconds after closing benediction/music ends.
- [ ] **Verify local recording file integrity** (ensure file size is > 5GB and plays back smoothly).
- [ ] Transfer recordings to the church media storage server / drive for Outdoor and Web teams.
- [ ] Power down cameras, cap all lenses, pack away loose HDMI/SDI cables cleanly.
- [ ] Hold a 5-minute team debrief: celebrate what went well and note 1 area of improvement.

## 4. Emergency Action Plan (Troubleshooting)

| Issue | Immediate Action | Secondary Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camera Signal Lost (Black screen)** | Switcher immediately cuts to Cam 1 (Wide) or graphic slide. | Check SDI/HDMI cable connection at camera and switcher. |
| **Stream Dropped / Internet Outage** | Do NOT stop local recording. Continue recording normally. | Restart OBS/vMix stream button. If router failed, switch streaming PC to mobile hotspot backup. |
| **Slide Operator Freezes / Wrong Lyric** | Clear slide immediately (blank lower third). Clean screen is better than wrong lyrics. | Operator re-aligns to current verse, then fades back in. |
| **Audio Distortion / High Buzz** | Pull down ambient room mics; check Master stream limiter. | Switch broadcast audio input to secondary backup aux send. |

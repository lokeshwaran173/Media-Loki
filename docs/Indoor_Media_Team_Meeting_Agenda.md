# FLAG Church - Indoor Media First Team Meeting Agenda
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Host & Leader:** Lokesh (Indoor Media Team Leader)  
**Meeting Length:** 45 - 60 Minutes  
**Tone:** Encouraging, Visionary, Practical, Spirit-Led

---

## 1. Meeting Objectives
1. Set a positive, inspiring vision for the Indoor Media Ministry under new leadership.
2. Honor the foundation laid by the previous team leader while introducing upgrades for excellence.
3. Walk the team through the **6 Action Checklists** and the **October Roster**.
4. Eliminate burnout by introducing apprentice pairings for 2nd and 3rd services.
5. Create an open forum to listen to the volunteers' needs, gear frustrations, and ideas.

## 3. Leader's Speaking Notes & Guide

### A. Opening & Vision (00:00 - 00:10)
* **Scripture Focus:** Colossians 3:23-24 (*"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters..."*)
* **Key Message:** 
  > *"Media in FLAG church is not just pushing buttons or turning on cameras. Every camera angle and every live stream is an open door for someone sitting at home who needs salvation, comfort, or hope. We are ministers of the Gospel through technology."*

### B. Honoring the Past & Stepping Forward (00:10 - 00:15)
* Express genuine gratitude for the previous leader and the groundwork already established.
* Emphasize that our goal now is to build **consistency, structure, and teamwork** so nobody feels overwhelmed or stressed on Sunday mornings.

### C. Introducing the 6 Checklists (00:15 - 00:30)
Present the [Indoor_Media_Team_Checklists.md](Indoor_Media_Team_Checklists.md) document. Emphasize:
* *"Checklists are not rules to control you; they are shields that protect you. When we follow a checklist, nobody gets blamed for a dead battery, a forgotten cable, or a late stream."*
* Specifically highlight:
  1. **Yashwant & Sam Davit:** Praise their dedication to gear charging and explain the new Saturday charging SOP.
  2. **Outside TV:** Explain how important the lobby/porch TV is for parents and overflow members who cannot sit inside.
  3. **Mic Batteries:** Stress the emergency 8-pack battery drawer so the preacher never loses voice.

### D. Solving Burnout & Announcing Pairings (00:30 - 00:40)
* Address Praveen directly: Appreciate his faithfulness in 2nd service and announce that he will no longer be running camera and switcher alone.
* Introduce the apprentice pairings (e.g., Sam Davit and Franklin assisting Praveen).
* Introduce the spot editing training with Joshua.

### E. Open Floor (00:40 - 00:50)
* Ask two specific questions:
  1. *"What is the most frustrating technical problem you face during a service?"*
  2. *"What equipment, cables, or accessories do you feel we are missing?"*
* Write their answers down in your leadership notebook.

### F. Closing Commissioning Prayer (00:50 - 00:60)
* Lay hands or extend hands in prayer over all media team members.
* Pray for steady hands, alertness of mind, protection from technical failures, and spiritual sensitivity during every service.

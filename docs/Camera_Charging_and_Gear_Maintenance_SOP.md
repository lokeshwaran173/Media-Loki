# FLAG Church - Camera Charging & Gear Maintenance SOP
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Assigned Leads:** Yashwant & Sam Davit (Camera Charging Incharges)  
**Applicable To:** All Indoor Media Crew Members

---

## 1. The Critical Mission of the Charging Incharge
A live church service cannot be paused or restarted because a battery died or an SD card ran out of space. 

As the **Charging & Equipment Incharges**, Yashwant and Sam Davit ensure that every camera, gimbal, wireless microphone, and monitor is powered, clean, and 100% ready before the worship team plays their first chord.

---

## 2. Master Equipment Charging & Power Schedule

### Saturday Evening Prep (Before 8:00 PM)
- [ ] **Camera Batteries (Main & Spares):**
  - Charge all primary camera batteries (Sony NP-F, Canon LP-E6, or camera-specific packs) to **100%**.
  - Ensure **at least 1 fully charged backup battery** per active camera is placed in the media bag.
- [ ] **Gimbal Battery / Handle:**
  - Charge the motorized gimbal (DJI Ronin / Zhiyun Crane) to **100%**. Gimbals draw extra power when cameras are not perfectly balanced.
- [ ] **Wireless Audio Transmitters & Receivers:**
  - Check battery levels on all wireless lapel/headset mic bodypacks.
  - Keep a fresh pack of **rechargeable AA / 9V batteries** tested and ready.
- [ ] **Field Monitors & Wireless Video Transmitters (if applicable):**
  - Charge NP-F970/750 battery plates for director monitors and wireless HDMI/SDI transmitters (Hollyland / Accsoon).

## 3. Sunday Morning Pre-Service Gear Inspection (T - 75 min)

```
[ Battery Verification ] ──> [ Lens Cleaning ] ──> [ SD Card Formatting ] ──> [ Rig Balancing ]
```

### Step 1: Battery Installation & Health Check
1. Insert batteries into Camera 1, Camera 2, and Gimbal.
2. Power on each unit and verify the on-screen battery percentage/bars.
3. If any battery is below **95%**, immediately swap it with a fresh spare and place the weak battery on fast charge.

### Step 2: Optical Cleanliness
* Inspect front lens elements on all cameras.
* Use a **blower bulb first** to remove dust/grit (never blow with your mouth; moisture spots the glass).
* Gently wipe in circular motions using a clean **microfiber cloth** and optical lens cleaning fluid.
* Check sensor/filter cleanliness under light.

### Step 3: SD Card Management & Formatting Standard
* **Golden Rule:** Never format an SD card on Sunday morning without confirming last week's files are backed up to the main drive!
* Once verified:
  1. Insert SD cards into their designated cameras.
  2. Format the card **in-camera** (do not format via computer, as in-camera formatting prevents card read/write errors).
  3. Verify remaining recording time (must show at least **3 hours** of available space in 1080p/4K).

### Step 4: Gimbal Balance Check (with Lokesh / Gimbal Op)
* Before powering on the gimbal:
  * Tilt axis balance check.
  * Roll axis balance check.
  * Pan axis balance check.
* *A properly mechanically balanced gimbal consumes 50% less battery and prevents motor shudder during worship.*

---

## 4. Post-Service Pack-Down & Storage Routine

At the conclusion of the 3rd Service:
1. **Power Down Sequence:** Turn off all cameras and monitors *before* disconnecting HDMI/SDI cables or removing batteries.
2. **Lens Protection:** Reattach lens caps to all camera bodies.
3. **Cable Discipline ("Over-Under" Wrap):**
   * Wrap all HDMI, SDI, and XLR cables using the standard **over-under roadie wrap** (never wrap tightly around your elbow; this breaks the internal copper core).
   * Secure cables with Velcro ties.
4. **Docking Station:** Place all drained batteries onto their respective chargers in the church media cabinet so they are ready for mid-week prayer meetings.
5. **Lock-Up:** Lock the media storage cabinet and hand the key to Lokesh / church administrator.

# FLAG Church - Indoor Media Team Action Checklists
**Church:** Full Life Centre AG Church (FLAG)  
**Department:** Indoor Media  
**Target:** Standardized duties for every team member before, during, and after service.

---

## 1. Camera Cleaning Checklist
**Assigned To:** 1st Cam & 2nd Cam Operators / Charging Incharge  
**When:** Every Saturday Evening & Sunday Morning at T - 75 min

- [ ] **Outer Body & Tripod Dusting:**
  - Wipe down camera cages, tripod legs, fluid heads, and cables with a clean microfiber cloth.
  - Check tripod pan/tilt locks and ensure quick-release base plates are firmly tightened.
- [ ] **Front Lens Element Cleaning:**
  - **Step 1:** Use an air-blower bulb to blow away loose dust and sand particles (never blow with your mouth).
  - **Step 2:** Use a soft-bristle optical lens brush to sweep away remaining debris.
  - **Step 3:** Apply 1-2 drops of optical lens cleaner onto a fresh microfiber cloth (never directly onto the glass) and wipe gently in expanding circular motions from center to edge.
- [ ] **Viewfinder & LCD Screen:**
  - Clean camera LCD monitors and flip screens. Remove smudges and fingerprints so exposure and focus peaking are clearly visible.
- [ ] **Sensor & Mount Check:**
  - Inspect for internal dust specks at narrow apertures ($f/8 - f/11$). If dust is on the sensor, notify the team leader immediately.
- [ ] **Post-Service Protection:**
  - Immediately reattach lens caps to all cameras before dismounting from tripods.

## 2. System Cleaning & PC Maintenance Checklist
**Assigned To:** Live in System Operators (Anand / Yashwant / Shyam Davit)  
**When:** Every Friday Evening & Sunday Morning at T - 60 min

- [ ] **Storage & Disk Space Verification:**
  - Check recording drives (`C:` and external SSD). Ensure at least **50 GB of free space** is available before service starts.
  - Move previous week’s recordings to the church backup drive / NAS to free up primary SSD space.
- [ ] **Cache & Temporary Files Cleanup:**
  - Clear OBS / vMix temporary replay and cache folders.
  - Empty Windows Recycle Bin and close background applications (Chrome, Discord, downloads, Steam, Spotify).
- [ ] **Hardware Dusting & Cable Check:**
  - Inspect PC tower cooling vents and fans; blow out dust buildup with canned air if needed.
  - Verify all rear USB, HDMI, SDI, and Ethernet cables are seated securely and not strained or bent.
- [ ] **System Reboot Routine:**
  - **Reboot the streaming PC 60 minutes before service.** Never stream on a PC that has been sleeping or running for multiple days without a fresh restart.
- [ ] **Windows Updates Frozen:**
  - Verify Windows Auto-Update is paused or set to active hours so it never triggers a restart mid-sermon.

## 3. Equipment Charging Checklist
**Assigned To:** Camera Charging Incharges (Yashwant & Sam Davit)  
**When:** Saturday by 7:00 PM & Sunday Morning T - 75 min

- [ ] **Primary Camera Batteries:**
  - Charge all main camera batteries (Sony NP-F, Canon LP-E6, or camera packs) to **100% full indicator**.
- [ ] **Spare Batteries (Minimum 1 per camera):**
  - Verify at least 3-4 fully charged spare batteries are labeled and placed in the top compartment of the media bag.
- [ ] **Gimbal Motor Battery / Handle:**
  - Charge the motorized 3-axis gimbal (DJI Ronin / Zhiyun) to 100%. Check power bank or handle grip indicator.
- [ ] **Field Monitors & Wireless Transmitters:**
  - Charge external director monitor batteries (NP-F750 / NP-F970) and wireless video receiver/transmitter units.
- [ ] **Charging Dock Protocol:**
  - Drained batteries from Friday Fasting Prayer must immediately be placed on the charging dock Saturday morning.
- [ ] **Power Strip & Extension Cords:**
  - Ensure the media desk master power extension board is switched on and firmly plugged into the church UPS socket.

## 4. Recharge the Mic Batteries Checklist
**Assigned To:** Charging Incharge & Audio / Live Tech  
**When:** Saturday Evening & Sunday Morning T - 60 min

- [ ] **Pastor's Wireless Lapel / Headset Mic:**
  - Inspect the transmitter bodypack. If using rechargeable AA cells, insert **fresh 100% charged batteries**.
  - If using standard alkaline AA batteries, ensure battery meter displays **full bars** (never use a battery below 3 out of 4 bars for the main preacher).
- [ ] **Worship Leader & Pulpit Handhelds:**
  - Check battery levels on all wireless handheld microphones used on stage.
- [ ] **Emergency Battery Caddy at Media Desk:**
  - Keep a dedicated plastic case containing **at least 8 fresh, tested AA and AAA batteries** at the media control table at all times.
- [ ] **RF Signal & Battery Meter Check on Receivers:**
  - Turn on all transmitters on stage. Check the receiver units at the sound rack:
    - Ensure RF link indicator is solid green (no dropouts).
    - Confirm the receiver displays a full battery icon.

## 5. Set up the Outside TV & Live Stream Verification Checklist
**Assigned To:** Assigned Media Volunteer / Floater  
**When:** Sunday Morning at T - 45 min to T - 20 min

> The Outside TV serves church overflow, parents with toddlers, welcome lobby, and porch areas. It must deliver clear video and balanced sound.

- [ ] **TV Power & Hardware Connection:**
  - Power on the outside TV display and verify the HDMI / SDI cable or wireless receiver (Chromecast / Apple TV / Cat6 HDMI extender) is connected firmly.
  - Secure loose outdoor cables with gaffer tape to prevent church members from tripping.
- [ ] **Live Feed Input Test (T - 30 min):**
  - Tune the Outside TV to the live feed source (Direct HDMI split from ATEM/Switcher OR the YouTube private countdown stream).
  - Verify video is sharp (1080p) and not stuttering.
- [ ] **Outdoor Audio Level & Speaker Calibration (T - 20 min):**
  - Walk outside to the TV location while worship team is doing their soundcheck.
  - Verify audio is loud and clear over ambient street and lobby noise.
  - Ensure there is no harsh distortion or ground buzz/hum on the outdoor TV speakers.
- [ ] **Sunlight & Glare Adjustment:**
  - Check TV screen brightness and angle. Tilt the screen downward or shade it if morning sun hits the display.
- [ ] **Mid-Service Check (15 mins into 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Service):**
  - Floater does a 60-second walk to the outside TV to confirm the pastor’s sermon is broadcasting clearly and the screen hasn’t gone to sleep.

## 6. Sunday Service Live Procedure (Master Execution SOP)
**Assigned To:** All Indoor Media Crew Members  
**Coordinated By:** Media Director & Switcher

```
[ T - 90 min ]  Call Time: Team arrival, devotional scripture, and prayer.
      │
[ T - 75 min ]  Hardware Setup: Turn on Switcher, Streaming PC, Cameras, and Outside TV.
      │
[ T - 60 min ]  Camera Check: White balance match (4500K-5600K), focus check, battery check.
      │
[ T - 45 min ]  Audio Sync: Test FOH aux send, ambient room mics, and wireless lapel levels.
      │
[ T - 30 min ]  Visuals & Slides: Load sermon slides, check lyric spellings, test lower-thirds.
      │
[ T - 15 min ]  Go Live: Start YouTube/Facebook stream with Countdown Video + Music Loop.
      │
[ T - 05 min ]  Comms Check: Final mic check with Camera 1, 2, and Gimbal.
      │
[ T - 00 min ]  SERVICE BEGINS: Switcher takes Cam 1 (Wide) as worship team opens.
      │
[ DURING ]      Cut on beat for praise; hold steady shots for preaching; track scripture.
      │
[ SERVICE END ] Fade to Outro Screen with Giving details and closing instrumental music.
      │
[ T + 05 min ]  1. Stop Live Stream  ➔  2. Stop Local Recording.
      │
[ T + 15 min ]  Verify recorded file on SSD (must play smoothly with clean audio).
      │
[ T + 25 min ]  Transfer master file to Spot Editor (Joshua) & Church Storage Server.
      │
[ T + 30 min ]  Pack-down: Cap lenses, over-under wrap cables, put batteries on chargers.
```

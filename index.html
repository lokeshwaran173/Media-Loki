                👉 How to get your FREE Firebase Database URL in 2 minutes (Step-by-Step Guide)
              
              

                
    
                  
  1. 
                    Open Firebase Console (sign in with
                    any free Google account).
                  
    2. 
                  
  2. 
                    Click + Add project, enter a name (e.g. flag-church-media), and click
                    Continue until the project is created.
                  
    3. 
                  
  3. 
                    In the left navigation menu, click Build ➔ Realtime Database ➔
                    click Create Database.
                  
    4. 
                  
  4. 
                    Select your nearest region (e.g. Singapore or United States), click
                    Next, then choose "Start in test mode" (allows read & write)
                    and click Enable.
                  
    5. 
                  
  5. 
                    Copy the Database URL displayed at the top of your database page (it looks like:
                    https://flag-church-media-default-rtdb.asia-southeast1.firebasedatabase.app or
                    https://flag-church-media-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com).
                  
    6. 
                  
  6. 
                    Paste that URL into the box above and click 🔗 Connect & Enable Cloud Sync.
                    That's it!
                  
    7.
